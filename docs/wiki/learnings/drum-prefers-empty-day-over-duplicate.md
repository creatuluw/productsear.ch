# Drum prefers empty day over duplicate

When the no-repeat rule skips the reader's last-seen type and no other generator succeeds, the drum deliberately publishes nothing that day rather than a repeat. This is intentional filler-avoidance consistent with the human-gated editorial philosophy.

Low practical risk: pulse, resurrections and tombola are data-driven and rarely all fail together.
